Our own product: ROSC, a shared record for savings groups.

Live on iOS and Android.

ROSCContributions, payouts and records for savings groups

ROSC started with a family savings group, the kind that usually runs on group chats and someone's notebook. Every turn, each member marks their share as sent, the person receiving confirms it, and the whole group sees the same record.

What's newVersion 1.1.0
  • Invite links that open straight to the right group
  • A group noticeboard for notes, reactions and pinned updates
  • Trust Score history, so members can see their record build
  • Owing groups that follow up missed payments with a clear balance
  • A calmer design in light and dark, with tighter security
Get it on Google Play rosc.packisher.com
