About this policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

This policy covers the Packisher website at packisher.com. Each of our apps has its own policy. For ROSC, see the ROSC privacy policy.

In short: this website has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies. We only receive what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the services you're interested in and your message.
  • Email: anything you include when you write to us directly.
  • Preferences: if you switch to dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser. It never reaches us.

What we don't collect

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't ask you to create an account.
  • We don't sell or share your information with marketers.

How we use it

We use your message only to reply to you and to discuss a possible project with you.

Who handles it

  • Resend delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and running.

Keeping and deleting

We keep project emails for as long as they're useful for the conversation or any work that follows. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.
