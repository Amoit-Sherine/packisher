Privacy

Last updated September 30, 2026

This policy covers the Packisher website at packisher.com. Our apps have their own policies. For ROSC, see rosc.packisher.com/privacy.

What we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you use the enquiry form, it opens your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

When you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use it

We use your message only to reply to you and to discuss a possible project. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Hosting

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and running.

Keeping and deleting

We keep project emails for as long as they are useful for the conversation or any work that follows. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.
